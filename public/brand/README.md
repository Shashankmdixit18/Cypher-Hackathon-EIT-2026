# Heritage Companion logo system

## Concept

The open arch represents a welcoming heritage gateway. The pin inside it points to the place being explored. Two upright travellers pass through together, expressing the companion role. The spare linework and restrained sandstone, marigold, ivory and indigo palette keep the mark warm and respectful beside a place of worship.

## Files

- `lockup-horizontal.svg` — primary horizontal logo with English, Hindi and the STORIES | CROWDS | SAFETY descriptor.
- `lockup-stacked.svg` — centered vertical lockup.
- `mark.svg` — transparent sandstone and marigold symbol.
- `app-icon.svg` — circular app avatar on ivory.
- `lockup-black.svg`, `lockup-white.svg`, `mark-black.svg`, `mark-white.svg` — single-colour artwork.
- `lockup-indigo.svg`, `mark-reversed.svg` — reversed artwork for indigo or dark backgrounds.
- `app-icon-512.png`, `app-icon-192.png`, `app-icon-64.png`, `app-icon-32.png` — raster app-icon exports.
- `lockup-horizontal.png`, `lockup-stacked.png` — large raster previews.

## Use rules

- Keep clear space around the mark equal to the height of one traveller head (about 7% of the mark width).
- Minimum mark size: 16 px for the icon; 120 px wide for the horizontal lockup.
- Use the symbol alone at small sizes. Do not add a tagline below 120 px lockup width.
- Use the standard version on ivory or light backgrounds; use `mark-reversed.svg` or `lockup-indigo.svg` on dark indigo.
- Keep the artwork flat and unshadowed. Do not stretch or recolour individual parts.

## Typography note

The lockup SVGs keep the wordmark as editable SVG text and specify Fraunces (Georgia fallback), Noto Serif Devanagari (serif fallback), and Inter (Arial fallback). For print handoff, open in a vector editor with those fonts installed and convert text to outlines before sending to a printer. The symbol itself is hand-built vector paths.
